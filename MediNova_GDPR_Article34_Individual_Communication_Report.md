## 3. Legal Basis — GDPR Article 34

GDPR Article 34(1) generally requires a controller to communicate a personal data breach to affected individuals without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms.

Article 34(2) requires the communication to describe the nature of the breach in clear and plain language and contain at least the information and measures referred to in Article 33(3)(b), (c) and (d).

These include:

1. The name and contact details of the Data Protection Officer or another contact point.
2. A description of the likely consequences of the breach.
3. A description of the measures taken or proposed to address the breach, including measures to mitigate possible adverse effects where appropriate.

The communication should be understandable and accessible to the intended recipients.

## 6. Proposed Patient Notification — Educational Draft

**Subject: Important Information About a Personal Data Security Incident**

Dear Patient,

We are writing to inform you about a personal data security incident involving MediNova.

Our investigation has confirmed that an account associated with a former employee was used to access patient records without authorisation.

We have identified approximately 180 affected patients in this fictional scenario.

The investigation is continuing to determine the full extent of the information involved. We will provide further information where appropriate.

Depending on the information accessed, potential consequences may include loss of confidentiality or misuse of personal information. The precise consequences remain under assessment.

Our security team is investigating the incident, reviewing the affected account and access controls, and determining appropriate steps to prevent similar incidents.

As a precaution, please remain alert to unexpected communications requesting personal or medical information. Do not provide sensitive details in response to unsolicited messages. If you receive a suspicious communication claiming to be from MediNova, verify it through a trusted contact channel.

If you have questions or concerns, please contact our designated privacy team using the verified contact information supplied in the final approved notification.

We regret the concern this incident may cause and remain committed to protecting personal information.

Yours sincerely,

**MediNova Privacy Team**

## 18. Conclusion

The fictional MediNova scenario demonstrates that regulatory notification and communication to affected individuals are separate GDPR obligations.

Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals and no Article 34 exception applies, the controller must generally communicate the breach without undue delay.

Effective communication should be clear, accurate and supported by appropriate review and documentation.
