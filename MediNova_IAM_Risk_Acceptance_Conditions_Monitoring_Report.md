## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents a fictional conditional risk acceptance decision and the ongoing monitoring requirements established for MediNova's employee offboarding risk.

Previous operating effectiveness testing identified delayed account deactivation. Subsequent corrective action retesting showed improved performance but identified one remaining exception.

For this training scenario, MediNova's authorised risk committee has conditionally approved temporary acceptance of the residual IAM risk for 30 days.

The acceptance is subject to ongoing monitoring, defined escalation requirements and review before expiry.

The related audit finding, GAP-001, remains open.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this report is to:

- Document the conditional risk acceptance decision.
- Define the approval conditions.
- Establish monitoring responsibilities.
- Identify escalation triggers.
- Specify review and expiry requirements.
- Maintain separation between risk acceptance and audit finding closure.

## 6. Monitoring Plan

### Daily Monitoring

The IAM team should review employee departures and identify accounts that remain active beyond the approved deactivation deadline.

### Weekly Reporting

The risk owner should receive a summary of:

- Overdue account deactivations.
- Unresolved exceptions.
- Privileged-account issues.
- Corrective action progress.
- Changes in risk exposure.

### Periodic Risk Review

The risk owner should reassess whether the accepted risk remains within the approved scope and conditions.

## 8. Escalation Triggers

The following events should trigger escalation or reassessment:

1. A former employee's privileged account remains active beyond the approved deadline.
2. A material increase in offboarding failures occurs.
3. A compensating control fails.
4. Unauthorised access is suspected or confirmed.
5. The risk changes materially.
6. An approval condition is breached.
7. The risk acceptance approaches expiry.
8. The acceptance expires without authorised renewal.

Escalation should follow MediNova's approved incident and risk governance procedures.

## 9. Illustrative Condition Breach

### Scenario

During the conditional acceptance period, the IAM team identifies a former employee's privileged account that remained active beyond the approved deadline.

### Assessment

The event breaches the condition requiring immediate escalation of privileged-account delays.

### Required Response

The GRC Analyst should:

1. Record the exception.
2. Preserve relevant evidence.
3. Notify the risk owner.
4. Escalate through the required governance process.
5. Assess the effect on the existing acceptance.
6. Determine whether additional security investigation is required.
7. Track the authorised management decision.

The analyst should not assume the existing acceptance automatically covers the condition breach.

## 10. Risk Acceptance Expiry Management

The approval is valid for a fictional period of 30 days.

Before expiry, the risk owner should:

- Review the current residual risk.
- Evaluate monitoring results.
- Assess outstanding exceptions.
- Review corrective action progress.
- Determine whether continued acceptance is justified.
- Obtain a new authorised decision if required.

**Automatic Renewal:** Not permitted under the assumed scenario conditions.

If approval expires without renewal, the risk should be escalated and handled under the organisation's applicable governance process.

## 13. Final GRC Recommendations

The GRC Analyst should recommend:

1. Retaining the documented approval decision.
2. Assigning clear monitoring responsibilities.
3. Maintaining daily and weekly evidence.
4. Escalating breaches of approval conditions.
5. Reviewing material changes in risk exposure.
6. Tracking corrective action progress.
7. Reassessing the risk before expiry.
8. Preventing unauthorised extensions.
9. Keeping GAP-001 separate from the risk acceptance decision.
10. Preserving the complete governance evidence trail.

## 14. Conclusion

This fictional MediNova assessment demonstrates that conditional risk acceptance requires continuing governance rather than passive acknowledgement.

Management approval must be supported by defined scope, conditions, monitoring responsibilities, escalation procedures and expiry controls.

A breach of an approval condition requires investigation and an appropriate governance response.

The related audit finding remains open until its separate closure requirements are satisfied.
