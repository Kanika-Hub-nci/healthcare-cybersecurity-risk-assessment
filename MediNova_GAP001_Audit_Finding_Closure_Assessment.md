## 1. Executive Summary

This report documents a simulated closure assessment of GAP-001, relating to weaknesses in MediNova's employee offboarding and access revocation process.

The original finding concerned the continued availability of a former employee's account after their departure.

The fictional organisation subsequently introduced a revised HR–IT offboarding procedure, investigated the previously identified account disablement exception and completed additional control effectiveness testing.

Ten follow-up samples met the assumed internal requirement to disable accounts within 24 hours of departure.

Based on the fictional evidence described in this assessment, closure is recommended, subject to formal approval by the authorised finding owner or designated reviewer.

**No actual remediation, testing or closure approval has occurred.**

## 2. Original Audit Finding

**Finding ID:** GAP-001

## 7. Residual Risk Considerations

The corrective actions are intended to reduce the risk of former employees retaining access after departure.

However, residual risk may remain because:

- Manual HR–IT communication may still experience delays.
- Certain applications may not be integrated with central identity management.
- Future process failures may occur.
- Sample-based testing does not guarantee complete control effectiveness.

The formal residual risk rating should be determined using MediNova's approved risk assessment methodology and supporting evidence.

No numerical residual risk score is asserted in this report.

## 10. Post-Closure Monitoring

Following formal closure, MediNova should continue periodic monitoring of employee offboarding controls.

Monitoring activities may include:

- Periodic reconciliation of HR departures against active accounts.
- Sample-based reviews of account disablement times.
- Investigation of late deactivations.
- Review of application-specific access removal.
- Reporting of recurring exceptions to management.
