# MediNova — Cybersecurity Risk Acceptance Request

**Risk Reference:** GAP-002  
**Risk Title:** MFA Exception for Emergency Administrator Account  
**Relevant Control:** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.8.5 — Secure Authentication  
**Risk Severity:** High (Illustrative)  
**Request Status:** Draft — Pending Assessment and Approval

## 1. Risk Description

One emergency administrator account in the fictional MediNova environment does not use the standard Multi-Factor Authentication method.

This may increase the risk of unauthorised privileged access to critical systems and sensitive healthcare information.

## 2. Business Justification

The IT Manager has proposed an exception because the account may be required during emergencies when normal authentication services are unavailable.

This justification requires review and validation.

## 3. Potential Impact

Unauthorised access to the emergency account could result in:

- Exposure of sensitive patient information.
- Unauthorised changes to critical systems.
- Operational disruption.
- Regulatory and reputational consequences.

## 4. Proposed Compensating Controls

1. Restrict emergency account access to authorised personnel.
2. Securely store and protect emergency credentials.
3. Monitor and alert on emergency account sign-ins.
4. Review account activity after each use.
5. Periodically test emergency access procedures.
6. Review whether stronger authentication alternatives are feasible.

These controls are proposed and have not been verified as implemented.

## 5. Risk Assessment

**Inherent Risk:** High — illustrative.

**Residual Risk:** To be assessed after reviewing the proposed compensating controls and their effectiveness.

**Risk Treatment Proposal:** Consider a time-limited exception only if the residual risk is within the organisation's approved risk appetite.

## 6. Evidence Required

- Emergency account inventory.
- Business justification for the exception.
- Documented risk assessment.
- Compensating control configuration.
- Monitoring and alerting records.
- Emergency access test results.
- Approval by the authorised risk owner.

## 8. GRC Recommendation

The exception should not be approved solely on the basis of an operational justification.

The authorised risk owner should review the risk assessment, business necessity, alternative safeguards, evidence and proposed review period before making a decision.

GAP-002 should remain open until the organisation's approved exception and finding closure requirements are satisfied.
