## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents a fictional review of a temporary risk acceptance concerning delayed deactivation of a privileged administrator account at MediNova.

In the training scenario, the designated Risk Owner approved a temporary acceptance on 1 October 2026, with an expiry date of 31 October 2026.

At the simulated review date, the underlying offboarding weakness remains unresolved, and no authorised extension has been obtained.

The original acceptance has therefore expired within the scenario.

**Assessment Outcome: EXPIRED — NO VALID EXTENSION RECORDED**

The risk remains open and requires reassessment, escalation and an authorised treatment decision.

No real organisational approval or expiry event is represented.

## 2. Background and Risk Description

**Risk Title:** Delayed Deactivation of Privileged Employee Account

**Risk Description:** A privileged account remains enabled beyond the permitted timeframe following employee departure.

**Potential Consequences:**

- Unauthorised administrative access.
- Exposure of sensitive organisational or patient information.
- Misuse of privileged permissions.
- Disruption of critical systems.
- Regulatory, financial and reputational consequences.

The scenario does not establish that unauthorised access or a personal data breach occurred.

## 14. Lessons Learned

Temporary risk acceptance should always include:

- Clearly defined scope.
- Documented authorisation.
- Justification for accepting the risk.
- Conditions and required safeguards.
- A specific expiry date.
- Periodic review requirements.
- Named accountability.
- A process for renewal, remediation or escalation.

Without these elements, exceptions may remain unresolved beyond their intended duration.
